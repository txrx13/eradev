## Part 5 — Adversarial Probe: "Is the Surface Clean but the Mental Model Still Code-Biased?"

This is the phantom-coherence check. The dangerous failure mode is: vocabulary was substituted but the underlying framework still encodes a code-specific mental model. Probe for residual code-mentality signals.

### Probe 1 — "compile" or its mental equivalent

- Direct `\bcompile\b` matches: **0**.
- Mental equivalent signals (verification-must-happen-mechanically-against-a-strict-parser): present, but reframed as "render/verify" and the Builder example 2 names `tectonic` (LaTeX compiler) which IS a compile step for an article. The concept survived correctly — LaTeX docs DO compile.
- **Residual risk:** NONE. "Compile" as a mental model is actually UNIVERSAL for text-discrete artifacts — a LaTeX paper compiles, an OpenAPI YAML schema parses, a Markdown chapter renders. The v9 refactor correctly generalized "compile" to "the domain's strict parse/render step."

### Probe 2 — "test pass" still in Validator's mental model

- Direct `\btest\b` matches: **0**.
- Mental equivalent signals: "validation" replaces "testing" throughout. Validator Key Rule 2 says "validation BEFORE fix" not "test BEFORE fix."
- Does the Validator STILL think of "passing validation" as a binary mechanical check? Yes — which is CORRECT. A citation either resolves or it doesn't. A LaTeX doc either compiles or it doesn't. A read-aloud pass either stumbles or it doesn't. The "binary mechanical" mental model IS the universal one for text-discrete artifacts (that's why OOD scope says AND of discreteness + objective verification).
- **Residual risk:** NONE. The "binary pass/fail" mental model is a feature, not a bug, per OOD scope.

### Probe 3 — "build" still anywhere

- Direct `\bbuild\b` matches: **0**.
- Mental equivalent signals: "produce," "generate," "render" throughout.
- "Build pipeline" replaced by "full pipeline." Concrete pipeline verbs are explicitly domain-substitutable per analyst_EN.md Rule 1 note.

### Probe 4 — "git" / version-control presupposition

- Direct `\bgit\b` matches: **0**.

### Probe 5 — Implicit code-first ordering in enumerations
