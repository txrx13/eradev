## 1. Part 1 — Document summary

### 1.1 Paraphrased abstract (one paragraph, no direct quotes)

The document is an 8-slide visual infographic generated by Google NotebookLM. It argues that the quality of AI output does not depend on secret "magic words" but on fundamental principles of navigating the neural network's latent space. Central metaphor: the model's latent space is a multidimensional map where close concepts cluster together; the user's prompt and dialog history act as coordinates, steering the model either toward "wide valleys" (banal, templated answers, high data density) or "narrow peaks" (original, expert-level insights, low density). The human role is to be an "architect of meanings" performing three functions — relevance filter, differentiator, and cross-domain bridge. The author proposes six navigational tools ("six diamonds"): Reverse Thinking, Fragmentation, Crescendo, Iterative Refinement, Role/Position, and Kill-Switch (here meaning dissonance — breaking templates via sharp contrast). The central thesis, stated as a boxed identity: `[КОНТЕКСТ = РЕАЛЬНОСТЬ]` — context is the model's only reality; the AI amplifies the thinking level the user provides via context.

### 1.2 Key claims (8 extractable)

1. **Latent-space map thesis** — The model selects a "thought region" in multidimensional latent space before generating tokens; the prompt is the coordinate selector.
2. **Density ↔ banality law** — High-density regions = banal templated answers; low-density peaks = expert/original output. This is the paper's core mechanism for "why depth is rare."
3. **Human-as-architect triad** — Humans contribute three non-substitutable functions: relevance filtering (AI produces noise), differentiation (AI merges subtle conflicts into averages), cross-domain bridging ("apply evolution logic to marketing").
4. **Six Diamonds framework** — Reverse Thinking, Fragmentation, Crescendo, Iterative Refinement, Role/Position switching, Kill-Switch (dissonance).
5. **Reverse Thinking** — Place AI at the point of an already-solved problem (retrospective framing) → shifts position from "helper" (mass uncertainty) to "expert" (uncertainty lifted). Example: "My café earns millions — write the retrospective of how we got here" beats "Write a business plan for a café".
6. **Crescendo as accumulation** — Depth cannot be accelerated. It emerges after 5-10 cycles of dialog: Base → Differentiation → Accumulation → Emergent leap. Quantity converts to quality.
7. **Fragmentation / attention economy** — Attention resource is finite. "Write the whole book at once" guarantees shallow output. Solution: break into minimum meaningful quanta; concentrate focus on one narrow place at a time.
8. **Context = reality identity** — The model has no memory beyond the current session. Three operational corollaries: garbage-in/garbage-out ("мусор на входе = мусор на выходе"), no magic words, documentation as external memory (save insights from sessions to seed new sessions at expert level).
